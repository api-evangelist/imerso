TL;DR Planning is a continuous effort throughout the project, part of the Construction PDCA cycle. Improve data collection and information access through Common Data Environments (CDEs), Mobile BIM apps, and onsite BIM Kiosks / BIM Stations. Look for monotonous, time-killing tasks that can be combined and automated through new technologies like onsite robots and new-generation laser scan inspections.
